Delete your GreenCrest account

GreenCrest by GreenCrest Facility Solutions · Last updated: November 25, 2026

You can ask for your GreenCrest account and the personal data linked to it to be deleted at any time. GreenCrest accounts are created by your organisation's administrator, so there are two ways to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Ask your administrator: contact the GreenCrest administrator at your employer or service provider and ask them to delete your account.
  2. Or email us: send an email to imharisuet@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my GreenCrest account". Send it from the email address you use to sign in to GreenCrest, so we can confirm the account is yours.

We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account details: name, email address, role and sign-in credentials.
  • Your profile photo (selfie).
  • Location records linked to your account.
  • Push notification tokens for your devices.

What may be kept

  • Work records your employer needs, such as completed checklists, work photos, shift records and reports. These are kept for as long as your organisation uses GreenCrest, then deleted within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Where possible, your name is removed from them.
  • Information we must keep to meet legal obligations.

See our privacy policy for more details about how GreenCrest handles your data.
