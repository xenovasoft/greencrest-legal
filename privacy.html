GreenCrest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how GreenCrest Facility Solutions ("GreenCrest", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the GreenCrest website at mygreenrest.app and the GreenCrest mobile apps for Android and iOS (together, the "Service").

GreenCrest is a business tool for facility and cleaning teams. Accounts are created by an administrator at your employer or service provider. The Service is not meant for the general public or for children.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, role (for example employee, supervisor, manager, quality control or customer), the company and locations you are assigned to, and your password, which is stored in encrypted form.
  • Profile photo (selfie): when an employee sets up their profile, the app uses the camera to take a selfie. It is used to identify team members inside the Service.
  • Work photos: photos you take with the camera or choose from your photo library to document completed work, issues or inspections.
  • Location: your device's precise location at the moment you complete a checklist, to confirm the work was done on site. We do not track your location in the background or when the app is closed.
  • Work records: checklists, shift times, approvals, work orders, issues, inspections, ratings, comments and reports that you or your team enter.
  • Technical data: basic information your device sends when it connects, such as IP address, device and browser type, and error logs, used to keep the Service secure and working.

The camera, photo library and location are only used after you give permission on your device. You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings, but some features (such as completing a checklist) may then not work.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: sign you in, show your assignments, record completed work, and produce reports for your employer and its customers.
  • To confirm that work was completed at the right place and time.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse, and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or the Service.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and the apps contain no ads.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your organisation: administrators, managers, supervisors and quality-control users in your organisation can see the work records, photos and locations connected to their teams and sites.
  • Customers of your organisation: customer accounts can see reports, photos and ratings for the sites they are responsible for.
  • Service providers: we use trusted providers to host and run the Service, including Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage) and Lovable (website hosting). They process data only on our behalf and under our instructions.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

4. How long we keep information

We keep account information for as long as the account is active. Work records, photos and reports are kept for as long as your organisation uses GreenCrest. When an organisation stops using GreenCrest, its data is deleted within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

5. Deleting your account and data

To delete your account and the personal data linked to it, follow the steps on our account deletion page: ask your organisation's GreenCrest administrator, or email us at imharisuet@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days. Some work records may be kept where your employer or the law requires it; if so, we will tell you.

6. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to data is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

The Service is for business use only and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for important changes we will notify users in the Service.
